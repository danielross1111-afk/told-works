Enquire

Begin here.

The path is short.

  1. i.
    A first email
    Tell Dan about the work — the brief, the people, what it needs to do. As detailed or as brief as you'd like.
  2. ii.
    A site visit
    Dan comes in person. He listens. No commitment in either direction at this point.
  3. iii.
    A clear answer
    Within a week of the visit, you'll have a yes or a no — and either way, an honest read on the brief.
