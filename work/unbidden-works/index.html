Outside Obligation

Unbidden Works.

Not commissions. Not on demand.

Not all of the work is asked for.

Each piece begins in recognition — another maker's work that has caught and held. Sometimes a work from history. Sometimes a craftsperson closer to hand. Sometimes a vision left unbuilt; sometimes a work already complete, met now through his own hands.

He doesn't make these because he must.
He makes them because he has to.

The Terms
